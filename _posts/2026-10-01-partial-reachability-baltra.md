## Key Idea

The goal of the internet is to allow separate networks to communicate in a decentralized manner, i.e, universal reachability. Today, it is observed, that due to various pressures, the internet only has partial reachability. The authors propose that this partial reachibility is fundamental to the internet. They claim the internet contains two kinda of persistent unreachability: peninsulas and islands. They develop a strong measurement methodology, detection algorithms, and quanity the existence of these unreachable entities. The main findings of their measurement campaign is that peninsulas, i.e., persistent unreachability is more common than Internet outages.


## Critique

The paper is very well written. The resarch question is clearly motivated and all necesary components are thuroughly defined. As thurough as the authors are, I think a clear overview of the *Trinocular*, *RIPE Atlas*, and *Ark* datasets could be placed in the introduction.
