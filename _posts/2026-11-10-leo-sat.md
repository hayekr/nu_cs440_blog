## Key Idea
The authors propose and emperically validate that current Congestion Control Algorithms (CCAs) designed for conventional terrestrial networks fail in Low-Earth Orbit (LEO) networks due to stochastic channel dynamics. They assert that the fundamental assumptions of CCAs are broken in LEO networks, as validated by measurements using 10 common CCAs on Starlink networks. This motivates their introduction of LeoCC.
