## Key Idea
The authors propose a novel congestion control algoritm (CCA) for real-time video applications. Real-time video applications require ultra-low latency to operate properly. Modern CCA's use delay or loss based estimators to determine tha channel congestion. These algorithms impart significant latencies in the system, due to slow feedback. Additionally, the Google Congestion Control (GCC) avoids queue buildup via a slow transmission ramp-up. The reason that modern CCA's do not work well with real-time video is due to the burstiness of the traffic. To solve this, the authors implement an overlay to CCA's called *Vidaptive*. It adds mock packets in the loop to allow algorithms like Copa to adapt with very low latency.

## Critique
Part of the evalution case is for cellular devices. *Vidaptive*'s method of using dummy packets is inefficient given energy constratints of cellular devices.


## Connections
This work relates well to S. McClure, et. al which expores in-network congestion signals, and the work from Z. Lai, et. al on LEO networks. It would be interesting to see a study on how *Vidaptive* can potentially benefit LEO networks.
