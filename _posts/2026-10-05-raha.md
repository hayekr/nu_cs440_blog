## Key Idea
RAHA is a tool developed to analyze degradation of traffic engineering with the goal of preventing outages. It treats the traffic engineering controller as a arbitrary optimizer. 

## Critique
The authors evaluate degradation against static traffic without modeling congestion control.

## Connections
Relates to the P-ECMP work by identifying degradation bottlenecks that P-ECMP can use to resolve.
